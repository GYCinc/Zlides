Grammar Rules

[COUNT: 2]

Was vs Has Been (Simple Past vs Present Perfect)

Aaron used a bridge metaphor: 'was' = completed/past, 'has been' = ongoing/connected to now. Yariel grasped it: 'It's a type of bridge.' Applied correctly: 'The claim was paused' (finished) vs 'The claim has been paused' (ongoing). Key error pattern: 'The doors are locked right now' — correctly identified the state implication of present perfect.

3rd person -s omission + subject-verb agreement

Persistent across all chunks: 'your image are freeze' (is frozen), 'he fall in one word' (he falls), 'he don't insist' (doesn't), 'each case is individual. So depend the moment' (depends on). This is Yariel's most systematic error, appearing in EVERY session. Self-correction ('has been or have been' → 'has been') shows monitor is active but inconsistent.
